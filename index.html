Phone Survey GPS

A field surveying application designed to work directly with the GPS/GNSS receiver built into your Android phone.

The interface introduces surveying concepts and workflows also found in NTRIP-RTK - GNSS Topographie, while Phone Survey GPS itself uses the phone's positioning hardware.

Main features

Survey points with the phone GPS, manage survey files, work with coordinate systems, view precision information, use field tools and export your data.

The free edition allows unlimited survey files and limits only the number of points acquired directly by Phone Survey GPS to 100 new points per survey. Imported points do not count toward this limit, and exports are not limited.

Professional GNSS / RTK

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi GNSS, NTRIP and professional RTK workflows are available in NTRIP-RTK - GNSS Topographie. Phone Survey GPS keeps several related controls visible for learning and interface continuity, but does not simulate RTK corrections.

Important: Phone Survey GPS accuracy depends on the phone hardware, satellite visibility and Android location quality. It does not guarantee centimetre-level accuracy.
