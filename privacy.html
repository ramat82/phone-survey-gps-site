Privacy Policy

Application: Phone Survey GPS

Last updated: 22 September 2026

1. Location data

Phone Survey GPS uses the device's precise location/GPS/GNSS data to provide surveying, positioning, mapping, coordinate and field-measurement functions requested by the user.

Location data is used for the application's surveying functions. Phone Survey GPS does not sell location data and does not use it for advertising.

2. Survey data and files

Survey points, coordinates, project information, imported files and exported files are handled for the functions requested by the user. Data is stored on the device unless the user explicitly chooses to export, share, copy or transfer it.

3. Permissions

The application may request permissions required for location access and file-related operations needed to import or export user data. Phone Survey GPS does not require Bluetooth or Wi-Fi GNSS connections for its phone-GPS surveying mode.

4. Purchases

If the user purchases the Full version through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive the user's full payment-card details.

5. Data sharing

Phone Survey GPS does not sell personal data. Survey data is not shared with third parties by the application unless the user deliberately exports or shares data using Android or another selected application/service.

6. Data retention and deletion

Survey data stored locally remains on the user's device until the user deletes it, removes the relevant files, clears the application's data, or uninstalls the application.

7. Children

Phone Survey GPS is intended as a surveying and field-work tool and is not specifically directed at children.
